How the matching works

Jobs come from two real sources: free job-search APIs (RemoteOK/Arbeitnow) and your own LinkedIn/Naukri job-alert emails (via "Check email job alerts" above) - both with real apply links back to the original posting. Every job is first ranked by a fast TF-IDF keyword estimate (grey badge on the Jobs page), and the actual AI model - Claude/Ollama, the same one that writes your tailored resumes - can score any job for real (blue badge) via "Score with AI", grounded in the same resume bullets it retrieved (shown on each job's own page). Tailoring and cover letters are grounded in that same retrieval, so the AI reorders/rewrites your real background instead of inventing anything new.
